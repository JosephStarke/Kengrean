# Active Context

The app was rebuilt (October 2026) from a single-page word quiz into a level-based course
with illustrated, voiced stories and many practice modes. The old gTTS audio, JSON configs
and text word lists were replaced by `content/*.js`; the old vocabulary lives on as topic
word packs in `content/wordbank.js`.

October 23, 2026 (later): restructured to follow Delicious Korean's teaching style and pacing —
A0 became "Learn Like a Baby" (listening only), Hangul became its own track, and A1/A2 gained
rounds, review openers, circling questions and review episodes. Word packs were replaced with
class-style core vocabulary.

## Next Ideas
